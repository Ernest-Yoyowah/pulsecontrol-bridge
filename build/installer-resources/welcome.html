PulseControl Bridge

Ernest Keyz Studios  •  v1.0.0

Welcome to PulseControl Bridge — the macOS companion app for PulseControl Mobile.

This installer will place PulseControl Bridge.app in your /Applications folder.

Once installed, launch the app and scan the QR code with PulseControl Mobile to start sending MIDI from your phone to any DAW or hardware on your local network.
